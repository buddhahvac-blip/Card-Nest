# CardNest affiliate program setup

Status: **infrastructure ready; network acceptance pending**

CardNest supports two initial marketplace partners because they are directly relevant to trading-card collectors:

- **TCGplayer Affiliate Program** (operated through Impact)
- **eBay Partner Network**

No affiliate link becomes public merely because a URL exists. The corresponding approval flag must also be `true`.

## 1. Apply to the networks

Use the official TCGplayer affiliate documentation/application and eBay Partner Network enrollment.

Do not place tax details, bank details, passwords, affiliate-network credentials, or private IDs in GitHub.

## 2. After acceptance, generate exact partner links

Create separate links for:

- Pokémon on TCGplayer
- Magic: The Gathering on TCGplayer
- Pokémon on eBay
- Magic: The Gathering on eBay

Use the exact HTTPS links generated by the network. Do not hand-edit tracking parameters.

## 3. Add the links only to Vercel environment settings

```
AFFILIATE_TCGPLAYER_APPROVED=true
AFFILIATE_TCGPLAYER_POKEMON_URL=<network-generated URL>
AFFILIATE_TCGPLAYER_MAGIC_URL=<network-generated URL>

AFFILIATE_EBAY_APPROVED=true
AFFILIATE_EBAY_POKEMON_URL=<network-generated URL>
AFFILIATE_EBAY_MAGIC_URL=<network-generated URL>
```

If a partner has not approved CardNest, leave its approval flag `false`.

## 4. Built-in safeguards

The site:

- validates affiliate destinations against partner-specific host allowlists,
- uses a CardNest redirect endpoint rather than exposing editable tracking logic,
- adds `rel="sponsored noopener noreferrer"`,
- shows a clear commission disclosure next to the partner shopping area,
- keeps official Pokémon/Wizards information links distinct from affiliate links,
- records only aggregate CardNest partner/category click events,
- does not treat affiliate clicks as behavioral advertising profiles,
- does not use third-party product imagery unless CardNest has the required rights.

## 5. Disclosure language

The public UI uses:

> CardNest may earn a commission if you buy through these partner links, at no extra cost to you.

Do not hide this disclosure in Terms or a footer. It must remain near the shopping links.

## 6. Product and brand separation

Affiliate revenue does not make third-party products CardNest products. The Discover area must continue to say CardNest is independent of Pokémon, The Pokémon Company, Wizards of the Coast, TCGplayer, and eBay.

Do not imply authentication, fulfillment, warranty, or endorsement by CardNest for an external marketplace listing.

## 7. Monitoring

Track:

- outbound clicks by partner/category,
- network-reported conversions,
- commissions actually paid,
- broken or expired links,
- refund/reversal rates if the network reports them.

Never fabricate conversion or revenue numbers inside Nest Mind.

## 8. Review cadence

Review affiliate links at least monthly and whenever a network changes terms, tracking rules, rate cards, or required disclosures.
