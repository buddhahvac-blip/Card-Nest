# CardNest Public Beta Launch Plan

CardNest is operating in **public beta** for discovery, testing, and collector feedback. Commercial sales remain closed.

The founder launch board tracks ten permanent operating points:

1. **Brand/name clearance** — commercial blocker until qualified clearance is recorded.
2. **Public beta first** — browsing can be public while real purchases remain disabled.
3. **Visible art quality** — known legacy badge/palette problems stay flagged until remastered.
4. **Five-second value proposition** — original guardians → collect/explore → build My Nest → future play.
5. **Security and founder controls** — sensitive tools and production actions stay server-authorized.
6. **Trust and support** — public support path, seller identity, privacy/request handling, and commercial terms precede sales.
7. **Collector-funnel measurement** — use privacy-conscious first-party signals, not invasive behavioral ads.
8. **Unit economics** — validate payment, art/model, hosting, support, refund, acquisition, and other costs before pricing.
9. **Third-party separation** — Pokémon/Magic discovery remains clearly separate from original CardNest IP and only becomes affiliate commerce after approval.
10. **50–100 collector validation** — use real beta behavior and opt-in feedback to prioritize art, packs, and gameplay instead of feature volume.

## Current commercial gate

`commercialSalesReady = false`

Real payments must remain disabled. Stripe runtime accepts test-mode configuration only and requires the explicit founder payment approval flag.

## Public beta purpose

The beta is intended to answer:

- Do visitors understand CardNest quickly?
- Which guardians and Themes earn intentional interest?
- Do collectors inspect cards, preview packs, create/use My Nest, and return?
- Which art or UI issues reduce trust?
- What should be improved before broader investment in all 369 cards?

Guardian Enforcer treats this document's corresponding `data/launch-readiness.json` as a protected launch policy.
