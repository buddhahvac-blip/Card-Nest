# NestRune audio sources

## Rune Dungeon world music

NestRune uses separate battle music for each Rune Dungeon world. All three tracks below are published on OpenGameArt under **CC0 1.0 / public-domain dedication**, so they may be used commercially without royalties or mandatory attribution. We still preserve creator/source information here for provenance.

### World I — Verdant Skywilds
- **Track:** Fairy Battles
- **Creator:** MintoDog
- **Source:** https://opengameart.org/content/fairy-battles
- **License:** CC0
- **Runtime file:** https://opengameart.org/sites/default/files/fairy_battles_bpm180_0.ogg
- **Use:** bright enchanted/fairy battle loop for Bloom, Tide and Mystic floors

### World II — Emberstorm Crucible
- **Track:** Hope (Orchestral battle music)
- **Creator:** MintoDog
- **Source:** https://opengameart.org/content/hopeorchestral-battle-music
- **License:** CC0
- **Runtime file:** https://opengameart.org/sites/default/files/hope_orchestral_battle_music_bpm165_0.ogg
- **Use:** bright orchestral action loop for Ember and Volt floors

### World III — Eclipse Runeheart
- **Track:** Heavy Boss Battle 2
- **Creator:** MintoDog
- **Source:** https://opengameart.org/content/heavy-boss-battle-2
- **License:** CC0
- **Runtime file:** https://opengameart.org/sites/default/files/heavy_boss_battle_2_bpm110_0.ogg
- **Use:** dark, heavy, loopable battle music for Shadow/Mystic elite floors and the Runeheart Boss

## Audio policy

- Music remains background-only and is ducked during ability/Special impacts.
- Players can mute Music + SFX from the battle HUD.
- No paid advantage, card odds or reward rules are tied to audio.
- Do not replace these tracks with copyrighted commercial game/music references without separate license review.
