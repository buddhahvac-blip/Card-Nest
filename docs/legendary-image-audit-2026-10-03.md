## Future Legendary master-art brief

Preserve each canonical creature and approved identity. Prefer a dramatic but readable pose, strong silhouette, story-rich habitat, high-contrast light and unmistakable Theme. Keep family-friendly original anatomy and imagery. Do not borrow protected characters, signatures, compositions, frames or visual identities from Pokémon, Magic, Lorcana or other franchises. New master illustrations require separate review; this change does not regenerate or alter approved source illustrations.
