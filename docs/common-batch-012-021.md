# CN1-012–021 Common Art Production Briefs

These are the next contiguous Season One cards after completed CN1-011. Keep the current CN1-001–011 Common masters as the hard style anchor.

## Non-negotiable visual standard

- Same premium Common language as CN1-001–011
- Cute/family-friendly but not preschool
- Soft polished painterly fantasy rendering
- One clear creature, full body or nearly full body
- Large expressive eyes where anatomically appropriate
- Strong silhouette at thumbnail size
- Rich Ember habitat, but less flashy than Rare/Epic/Legendary showcase art
- Clean composition; no excessive particles, gold, bloom, or ornamental clutter
- Raw master art only: no card number, rarity, stats, logo, CardNest wordmark, frame, UI, or watermark
- Vertical 3:4 composition preferred
- Preserve canonical order, names, themes, classes, habitats, and stats
